(05) Questions

Apps, answered.

How much does an app cost?+

It depends on features and platforms. A focused first version typically ranges from ₹6 lakh to ₹25 lakh. You'll get a fixed quote after discovery.

Native or cross-platform?+

For most businesses, cross-platform (one codebase for iOS and Android) is faster and more affordable. We'll recommend what fits your product.

Who owns the code?+

You do. The code, designs and app store accounts are yours from day one.

Do you maintain the app after launch?+

Yes. Ongoing support covers updates, OS changes, bug fixes and new features.
