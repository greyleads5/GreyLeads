(09) Questions

FAQs, answered honestly.

Still wondering something? Ask us on WhatsApp. A real person replies, not a bot.

What exactly does GreyLeads do?+

We're a full-service digital agency. We build websites and apps, design brands and creative ads, and run the marketing that brings in customers: lead generation, paid ads, SEO, AEO and social, all under one roof.

How much does it cost?+

Marketing retainers start from ₹40,000 a month. Websites and apps are quoted as fixed-price projects after a short discovery call, so you know the full cost before we start.

How soon will I see results?+

Paid campaigns usually show clear signals within the first 30 to 45 days. SEO and AEO compound over three to six months. We set honest expectations in the first call, before you commit to anything.

What is AEO, and do I need it?+

AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation) gets your brand mentioned when people ask AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini or Google's AI answers for a recommendation. More and more buyers start their search there, so if you're not in those answers, a competitor is.

Do I own my website, ad accounts and data?+

Always. Your website, app code, ad accounts, creatives and data are yours, in your name, from day one.

Is there a long-term contract?+

A three-month starting period for marketing, then month to month. We'd rather keep clients with results than with contracts.
