(01) Our story

Built for brands that refuse to blend in.

GreyLeads started with a simple frustration: too many businesses were paying for marketing that looked busy but never showed up in the bank account. Beautiful posts, rising follower counts, and still no qualified inquiries.

So we built the agency we wished existed. One team that designs the website, builds the app, creates the ads, runs the campaigns and wires up the CRM, and is measured on one thing: the leads and revenue it brings in.

Today we work with startups, D2C brands, education companies, B2B firms and local businesses in the US, Europe and India, from our studio in Bellandur, Bengaluru.
