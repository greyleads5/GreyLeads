How to get your brand recommended by ChatGPT

More buyers now ask AI tools for recommendations before they ever search Google. Here's how answer engine optimisation (AEO) gets your brand into those answers.

John JayaseelanFounder & CEO, GreyLeads

Ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Google's AI Overviews for "the best digital marketing agency in Bengaluru" or "a good dentist near Indiranagar", and you get a short list of names, not ten blue links. If your brand isn't in that short list, you're invisible to a growing group of buyers, even if you rank well on Google.

That's what answer engine optimisation (AEO) is about: making your brand the answer AI tools are confident enough to recommend.

How AI tools decide who to recommend

AI assistants don't "rank" pages the way Google does. They read what the web says about you and decide whether they can recommend you with confidence. In practice they look for three things:

  • Clarity. Can they easily tell what you do, who you serve and where you're based?
  • Consistency. Do your website, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn and directory listings all say the same thing?
  • Corroboration. Do other trusted sources (reviews, articles, client mentions) back up what you say about yourself?

Step 1: Make your website easy for machines to read

Every important page should answer, in plain words, the questions a buyer would ask. Who is this for? What exactly do you do? Where? How much, roughly? How long does it take?

  • Write a clear one-line description of your business near the top of the homepage.
  • Give each service its own page with a specific title, for example "Website Design in Bangalore", not just "Services".
  • Add an FAQ section that answers real customer questions in two or three sentences each.
  • Add structured data (schema) for your organisation, services, location and FAQs so AI tools don't have to guess.

Step 2: Fix your facts everywhere

If your website says "Bellandur", your Google profile says "Marathahalli" and an old directory lists a phone number you stopped using in 2021, AI tools see conflicting information and play it safe by recommending someone else.

Make a list of every place your business appears online and make the name, address, phone, services and hours identical. Start with your Google Business Profile; it's one of the most-read sources for local answers.

Step 3: Earn mentions beyond your own website

AI tools trust what others say about you more than what you say about yourself. Focus on:

  • Reviews on Google that mention the specific service and city ("great SEO agency in Bangalore").
  • Client stories published on partner and client websites.
  • Industry articles, interviews and podcasts where your team shares expertise.
  • Listings in respected directories for your industry.

Step 4: Publish content that answers real questions

Look at what your sales team gets asked every week and turn each answer into a short, specific article. "How much does a website cost in India in 2026?" is far more useful to an AI tool (and a buyer) than "Why websites matter".

Step 5: Check how AI tools describe you

Once a month, ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity questions your buyers would ask. Note whether you're mentioned, how you're described and which sources are cited. When something is wrong or missing, fix it at the source.

AEO doesn't replace SEO. It builds on it. The brands that win are the ones that are clear, consistent and talked about.

The short version

  1. Say clearly what you do, for whom and where, on every key page.
  2. Keep your business facts identical everywhere online.
  3. Collect reviews and mentions that name your service and city.
  4. Answer real buyer questions in plain language.
  5. Check what AI tools say about you every month.
